D0MD | In personUse external website/tool for withdrawal score. Consider SW referral.
D1MD | TM or in personRepeat PAWSS or withdrawal score. Review need for medications.
48hRN | In person preferredWithin 2 days after taper start. Phone is okay if patient is well known and symptoms are well controlled.
1 weekMD | TM or in personReassess withdrawal resolution, relapse prevention, supports, and SW needs.
Day 10RN | TM or in personCheck adherence, symptoms, safety, and support needs.
Day 14MD | TM or in personPhysician reassessment. RN follow-up in between as needed. SW PRN.
Transfer to ED / higher level of care

Escalate for severe/rapidly escalating symptoms, seizure, hallucinations/confusion, persistent vomiting, unstable vitals, oversedation/respiratory depression, suicidal ideation, return to alcohol during active withdrawal, inability to maintain oral intake, or unsafe home/support situation.
